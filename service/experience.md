# Our taxi service

Abgindon Taxi Solutions provides taxi services to customers. Our product
direction is AI-first, with experiences for three groups:

| Role | Customer need | Proposed AI capability |
|---|---|---|
| Driver | Understand a contract and current earnings | Ask questions through Gemini or Copilot |
| Manager | Review productivity and absence | Explain dashboard trends |
| Owner | Understand fleet performance | Summarize the fleet overview |

The repository begins **before** these AI integrations. The
`workshop/` files are example inputs to introduce one change at a time,
not evidence that a service is running. No application, dashboards,
employee records, runtime service, or credentials are included. Trust
must verify any future real deployment before approving its claims.
